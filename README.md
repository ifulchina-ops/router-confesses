A one-line confession for make.fun's "The Router Confesses" bounty ($2, top 10, 1st $0.64). The home Wi-Fi router dies at the exact moment a movie reaches its climax — and admits it did it on purpose. After nine years of carrying the house, the router wanted one cliffhanger of its own. The detective was about to name the killer; the router decided the silence was its turn to speak. The winning line becomes the house router's official apology, printed on the bounty page.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

It writes the confession to `/output/entry.txt` (one line, 134 characters).
